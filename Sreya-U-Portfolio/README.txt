Sreya U. | Creative & Digital Marketing Portfolio

1. In Windows, right-click Sreya-U-Portfolio-Codex.zip and choose Extract All.
2. Choose your Desktop as the destination.
3. Open the Sreya-U-Portfolio folder and double-click index.html to view it.
4. To edit the portfolio in Codex, open Sreya-U-Portfolio as a local project.
   index.html contains the page content and styles. Keep media beside it.

The four videos and their preview images are included. An internet connection
is used for Google Fonts and external links.
